How these surfaces were made
  • Radiocarbon dates from XRONOS, p3k14c and NeoNet, calibrated with IntCal20 and grouped into occupation episodes per site. Graves are kept.
  • Presence is recorded by ten open datasets (lipid residues, Rigaud 2018, Dolbunova 2023, Windler's Spondylus, dated barbed points, BDA, MedAfriCarbon, Rotunno & Crema 2025, DEFC, DObsiSS) and Jordan et al. 2016's earliest-pottery table for Africa or implied by a culture or stage label. Absences come from labels, except pottery absences in North Africa, which MedAfriCarbon and Rotunno & Crema record.
  • Each slice is its keyframe year ± 500 years. A cell's presence is the kernel-weighted share of occupations with the material, pulled toward the slice mean: f = (Σ wKy + f₀) / (E + 1).
  • Cells are masked below an evidence of 1.2, under ice, and for bone and antler where topsoil pH is below 5.5.
  • Firmness: share of 200 bootstrap runs (occupations resampled, one date drawn from each) in which a cell is at or above 50%.
  • Full notes: data/isostyle/METHODS.md and data/isostyle/audit/AUDIT.md.
