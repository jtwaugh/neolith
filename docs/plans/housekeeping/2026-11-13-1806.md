## Running the master critic again

The mechanical pass, about 3 minutes (it opens an app window behind your others):

```sh
make state-all        # = make state-visual, make state-ui, make state
```

| Command | What it does | Time |
|---|---|---|
| `make state` | Tests, validators, isostyle evals, terrain screens, map report, famous sites, research debt, critic records → `data/state/STATE.md`. Exits 1 only on a gated row. | ~90 s |
| `make state SKIP=cargo,evals` | Same, skipping the slow parts | ~10 s |
| `make state-visual` | 9 fixed views rendered headless, compared with the last accepted renders | ~30 s |
| `make state-visual GOLDEN=1` | Accept the current renders as the new baseline (after looking at them) | ~30 s |
| `make state-ui` | Click accuracy, crowding, camera and UI movement, through the harness | ~50 s |

Read `data/state/STATE.md`. The "Change" column is the delta against the last run.

The agent critics are the expensive judgement pass. Run them after a batch of fixes, not after every one. In Claude
Code, ask for them by name. They read `STATE.md` first and end with a record in `data/state/critics/`:

- "run the gsg-critic": overall look and feel against shipped strategy games (scored, ~1 h)
- "run the greatest-hits-critic": the famous sites in the running app (scored)
- "run the terrain-critic": wrong or odd biomes
- "run the smooth-critic, then `python -m isostyle evals --check` its claims": artefacts in the speculative fill
- "run the gap-critic on <layer>" / "run the exotic-critic on <place>": research and speculation plans

Then rerun `make state` so the new critic records show in its Critics table.
