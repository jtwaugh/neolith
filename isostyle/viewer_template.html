How these surfaces were made
  • Each slice is its keyframe year ± 500 years. Radiocarbon dates (XRONOS, p3k14c, NeoNet) are calibrated with IntCal20 and grouped into occupation episodes per site; graves are kept.
  • Three kinds of evidence point: dated occupations, off-site pollen cores (Neotoma), and individual ancient genomes (AADR). Each layer uses one kind; the evidence view shows how many are within reach.
  • A cell's value is the kernel-weighted share of evidence points with the trait, pulled toward the slice mean: f = (Σ wKy + f₀) / (E + 1). Open land is a published 1° reconstruction, looked up per cell.
  • Cells are masked below an evidence of 1.2, under ice, and for bone and antler where topsoil pH is below 5.5.
  • Firmness: share of 200 bootstrap runs (evidence resampled, one date drawn from each) in which a cell is at or above the middle level.
  • Presence-only layers (no source records absence) show where something is recorded, not where it was absent. Sources and licences for each layer are under the layer's notes; full notes in data/isostyle/METHODS.md.
