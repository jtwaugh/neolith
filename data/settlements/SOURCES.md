# Sources for `data/settlements/`

Generated by `make settlements`; edit `[settlements.citations]` in
`pipeline/config.toml`, not this file.

## Datasets

Cite each dataset below that the sites you use list in their `sources`
property, plus the calibration curve.

### XRONOS (`xronos`)

- **Cite:** Roe, J., Schmid, C., Ebrahimiabareghi, S., Heitz, C. & Hinz, M., 2025. XRONOS: An Open Data Infrastructure for Archaeological Chronology. Journal of Computer Applications in Archaeology 8(1): 242–263. https://doi.org/10.5334/jcaa.191
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0
- **URL:** https://xronos.ch
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-12 (103 files, hashes in `provenance.json`)
- **In the output:** 8116 sites, 50986 dates
- **Note:** XRONOS compiles dates from other databases and publications and asks that those are cited too; see the last section.

### p3k14c, release 2025.07 (`p3k14c`)

- **Cite:** Bird, D., Miranda, L., Vander Linden, M. et al., 2022. p3k14c, a synthetic global database of archaeological radiocarbon dates. Scientific Data 9, 27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01118-7
- **Licence:** The article is CC BY 4.0. The repository states CC BY 4.0 for text and figures and MIT for code, and gives no separate licence for the data file.
- **URL:** https://github.com/people3k/p3k14c
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-12 (1 file, hashes in `provenance.json`)
- **In the output:** 265 sites, 1316 dates
- **Note:** Only dates whose lab number XRONOS does not have. The file is the "scrubbed and fuzzed" one.

### NeoNet (Mediterranean), version 2 (`neonet`)

- **Cite:** Mazzucco et al. 2021, NeoNet radiocarbon database version 1, Pisa: MOD. https://doi.org/10.13131/archelogicadata-yb11-yb66. Data paper: Huet, T., Cubas, M., Gibaja, J.F., Oms, F.X. & Mazzucco, N., 2022. NeoNet Dataset. Radiocarbon Dates for the Late Mesolithic/Early Neolithic Transition in the North Central-Western Mediterranean Basin. Journal of Open Archaeology Data 10: 3. https://doi.org/10.5334/joad.87
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA according to the Pisa repository; CC BY 4.0 according to the data paper.
- **URL:** https://github.com/zoometh/neonet
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-12 (1 file, hashes in `provenance.json`)
- **In the output:** 150 sites, 498 dates
- **Note:** Only dates whose lab number is not already in hand. The file is the version c14bazAAR points to (2023-12-08).

### NeoNet Atlantic, version 1 (`neonetatl`)

- **Cite:** Huet, T., Basílio, A.C., Carvalho, A.F., Cubas, M., Gibaja, J.F., López-Romero, E., Oms, F.X. & Mazzucco, N., 2023. NeoNetAtl radiocarbon database, Pisa: MOD. https://doi.org/10.13131/unipi/z332-ye56
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA according to the Pisa repository.
- **URL:** https://github.com/zoometh/neonet
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-12 (1 file, hashes in `provenance.json`)
- **In the output:** 109 sites, 363 dates
- **Note:** Only dates whose lab number is not already in hand. The author list is as given by c14bazAAR.

### Hand entries (`curated`)

- **Cite:** `data/settlements/curated.geojson` in this repository, carried over from the 2023 `settlements.geojson`.
- **Licence:** Project's own.
- **In the output:** 8 sites
- **Note:** Names, founding years and populations entered by hand without recorded sources. The populations are placeholders.

### IntCal20 calibration curve (`intcal20`)

- **Cite:** Reimer, P.J., Austin, W.E.N., Bard, E. et al., 2020. The IntCal20 Northern Hemisphere radiocarbon age calibration curve (0–55 cal kBP). Radiocarbon 62. https://doi.org/10.1017/RDC.2020.41
- **Licence:** Not stated in the file.
- **URL:** https://intcal.org/curves/intcal20.14c
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-11 (1 file, hashes in `provenance.json`)
- **Note:** Used to calibrate every radiocarbon date.
